## Behavioral evidence

- Linked worktrees share one clone-local repository identity; independent
  clones do not. Worktree role confusion fails closed.
- Fingerprint v1 binds HEAD, base, index, tracked state, admitted untracked
  state, rename/delete, mode, symlink, binary content, normalized paths, and
  deliberate product exclusions.
- Approval, proof, risk acceptance, and verification bind the exact session,
  revision, accepted diff, fingerprint, claim set, and reviewer lineage. A
  later mutation makes prior records stale.
- Concurrent executor admission allows one owner. Reassignment increments the
  fencing token and rejects superseded writes.
- Path, command, environment, secret, network, process, resource, output,
  Git-authority, state-root, traversal, and symlink escapes fail closed.
- Session worktrees live under the external state root, survive manager
  restart, retain every nonterminal state, and clean up only after terminal or
  explicit aborted state.
- Tier 0-3 requirements are cumulative. Shared prompt, conversation, or
  conclusion is not independent verification. Tier 2 same-lineage degradation
  requires a named risk owner and audit reference; Tier 3 has no automatic
  override.
- Publication prepares and validates a candidate commit, affected-path ledger,
  base backup, and restore plan before authoritative mutation. It serializes
  the repository, revalidates clean/base/path preconditions, advances the
  target ref with compare-and-swap, and verifies a clean final checkout. A
  second stale session receives `publication_rebase_required`.
- `approve` persists authority only with fresh proof, fresh verification, and
  a satisfied challenge. `status` and `inspect` projections never gain
  mutation authority. Abort persists before cleanup.
